# Licensed inputs for the authored reconstruction

Recorded 2026-09-29. Game layout, progression, dialogue, geometry assembly, shaders and authoring scripts are produced in this workspace. Selected environment and anatomy inputs are third-party CC0 assets; they are **not original meshes created here and are not recovered Nintendo game data**.

- [Rock Face 01 — Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_face_01), Dario Barresi: scanned rock geometry and diffuse, normal and packed surface textures.
- [Rocky Terrain 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/rocky_terrain_02), [Cobblestone Floor 08](https://polyhaven.com/a/cobblestone_floor_08), [Slab Tiles](https://polyhaven.com/a/slab_tiles), [Medieval Blocks 05](https://polyhaven.com/a/medieval_blocks_05), Poly Haven: selected 2K diffuse, OpenGL normal and AO/roughness/metal maps. ## Reference photography only

The [National Park Service photograph of Evans Plunge](https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/011d7b74-155d-4519-3ee0-6e6bec9911b8), also [catalogued by Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Caver_Karen_Rosga_sits_on_the_edge_of_Evans_Plunge,_one_of_Wind_Cave%27s_lakes._(011d7b74-155d-4519-3ee0-6e6bec9911b8).jpg), is retained as a visual reference in `production/reference-assets/evans-plunge-nps.jpg`. It is not a game texture or runtime asset. The catalogue records it as a US government public-domain photograph.

Observed features: irregular layered rock, undercuts and fractures; small wet highlights; a visible rocky bed through clear foreground water; much darker unlit distance. These observations guide the study. They do not establish that the current render achieves the same realism.

Two additional public-domain Met Open Access photographs are reference-only: [incised leather boot 90.5.36b](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/443620) and [early 19th-century leather boots 13.49.37a, b](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/98235). Their period and function differ from the fictional character; they inform leather construction, not a claim of historical costume accuracy. Observed features include the sole-to-upper seam, shaped heel and toe, overlapping edges, rear/side joining seams, a thin rolled opening, creases around the flexing upper and irregular worn gloss. The present character study lacks much of this construction and material detail.

## Reproduction

From `ocarina-of-time/`:

```sh
python3 production/authored/fetch_reference_assets.py
blender --background --python production/authored/make_link_anatomical.py
```

The fetch script verifies every recorded SHA-256, preserves existing files and extracts only selected members from the system asset pack. It does not fetch ROMs. The Blender source retains packed images, rig and ten motion clips. `make_equipment.py` authors the sword, scabbard and wooden shield separately; `production/authored/equipment/equipment.blend` is their editable source. Garment microstructure textures and equipment grain are generated by these authoring scripts. In-game motion is integrated; final character quality remains pending.

## Adult anatomy and Temple of Time source update

The adult model `game/assets/link_adult.glb` and its editable `production/authored/adult-link-study/link-adult.blend` use the same pinned CC0 base and rig, with `caucasian-male-young.target` and `universal-male-young-averagemuscle-averageweight.target` from the same revision. Exact input bytes, URLs and SHA-256 are added to the reference manifest. Child-authored garments are refitted using body-vertex correspondence to the adult morph; this is an authored adaptation of licensed anatomy, not an original anatomical mesh. The retained MakeHuman code/data license distinction applies.

Recreate it with:

```sh
blender --background --python production/authored/make_link_anatomical.py -- --adult --no-render
blender --background --python production/authored/make_master_sword.py
python3 production/authored/make_temple_materials.py
```

The stone-map generator requires Python with NumPy and Pillow. It creates nine deterministic color/normal/roughness maps in `game/assets/temple/` from mathematical grain and masonry patterns. The Master Sword and its scabbard are authored geometry and material definitions; editable source is `production/authored/master-sword/master-sword.blend`. No recovered game assets or downloaded sword models are used.

The [Durham Cathedral nave photograph](https://www.durhamcathedral.co.uk/explore/the-cathedral-building-and-grounds/the-nave) is reference-only; source URL, a 1240 px image URL and SHA-256 are retained in `temple-reference-manifest.json`. No license for runtime redistribution is assumed. The image itself is retained only under `production/reference-assets/time-temple-20260929/` and is excluded from the source ZIP. Observed massive supports, paired arches, window light and stone joints guide the scene; current renders remain below photographic realism.


## Graveyard and adult equipment - 2026-09-29

`make_hookshot.py` authors the Hookshot, grappling head and Hylian Shield. `make_gravestone.py` authors the carved tablet, plinth, weathering maps and lettering. Editable Blender source and runtime GLB are preserved; these are not recovered ROM data.

Inspected photographs: [The Met cranequin, 1986.205](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/24926), whose page marks the image Public Domain, and [Jason Church / NPS headstone example](https://home.nps.gov/articles/000/preservation-matters-resetting-veteran-s-headstone.htm). Photographs remain reference-only in `production/reference-assets/graveyard-20260929/`; their pixels are not runtime textures. URLs and SHA-256 are in [graveyard-reference-manifest.json](graveyard-reference-manifest.json). NPS Preservation Brief 48 supplied construction descriptions, but its PDF screenshots failed to load; no visual observation is attributed to those pages.

Observed mechanical details include the crank axle, retention fasteners, casing thickness, grip wrapping and varied metal reflections. Observed stone details include the rounded weathered shoulder, carved relief, vertical discoloration, grime at grade and soil contact. Current runtime surfaces, characters, enemy anatomy, flowers, lighting and material variation still fall short of these references.

Reproduction from `ocarina-of-time/`:

```sh
blender --background --python production/authored/make_hookshot.py
blender --background --python production/authored/make_gravestone.py
python3 tools/authored.py import
python3 tools/authored.py verify --graveyard --fixture --metal
```

Soil and masonry reuse the previously recorded Poly Haven CC0 inputs. ReDead use the existing adult anatomy with a provisional material and pose; Dampe and villagers retain provisional character derivatives. These are not final character reconstructions.
